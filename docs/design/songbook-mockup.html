Songbook: design mockup

Look and feel only. The songs are from your library and the chord names are the confirmed ones from the app's data, but the tempos, the key on the analysis screen and its progress are placeholders, not real results. Screens 5–7 use an invented example song with made-up lyrics and chords. Screen 7 brings in speedy-pitcher: speed and pitch of the real recording, loops and the speed trainer; with the pitch at −2 the chord lane is shown transposed. Yelp-inspired structure (bordered cards with soft shadows, outlined secondary buttons, pill filters, a strong primary button, list rows with art on the left) in Catppuccin colors: Mocha (dark, default) and Latte (light). Mauve is the brand and primary action, peach marks "now playing", and the finger colors map to peach p, blue i, green m, pink a.
